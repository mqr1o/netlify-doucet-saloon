Our Story

Castro Valley's Best-Kept Secret

Doucet Saloon has been the beating heart of Castro Valley since 1991. Tucked on Rutledge Road, this third-generation family bar isn't trying to be anything it isn't — and that's exactly what keeps people coming back.

Whether you're rolling in for a cold one at 7AM or shooting pool past midnight, you'll find the same friendly faces, the same ice-cold Bud long necks, and the same welcoming vibe that makes this place feel like home the moment you walk through the door.

Cash bar. No pretense. Just good people, great drinks, and the kind of neighborhood magic that corporate bars can never manufacture.
